About

Planning and design, grounded in data

With eight years of experience in urban and transportation planning, I bring expertise in data analysis and clear, compelling communication. I create before-and-after 2D and 3D visualizations to illustrate proposed solutions and develop articles and reports that make complex issues accessible to diverse audiences.

Beyond planning, I have 10+ years of experience in community engagement and research, collaborating with public and private organizations nationwide. I earned my Bachelor's in Sustainability and the Built Environment (Geodesign Specialization) with a Minor in Landscape Architecture from the University of Florida, where I honed my skills in spatial analysis and design-thinking to tackle urban challenges.

Passionate about sustainability, social equity, and data-driven decision-making, I bring a proven track record of leadership and entrepreneurship. I work with clients across the U.S. to create impactful, people-centered solutions.
